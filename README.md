## Final conclusion summary

The five think-aloud transcripts suggest that participants found the redesigned MCD experience clearer to scan and expected some browsing tasks to be easier. In the original experience, participants described difficulty finding MyM offers, locating the McDelivery menu, and noticing product details such as price, availability, and offer conditions. In the prototype, they responded positively to the MyM search option, clearer menu access, and product cards with more visible information.

Participants also raised questions about offer conditions and whether the prototype’s controls worked as expected. Overall, the transcripts point to a positive direction for the redesign, particularly for finding and comparing items. Participants’ spoken feedback supports improved clarity and discoverability, while also showing that some details and interactions need to be checked during use.
